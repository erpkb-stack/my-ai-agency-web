Demo · synthetic dataData extraction

Statement reader for loan underwriting

Reads multi-page bank statements and prepares income and balance figures for an underwriter to confirm.

The problem
Underwriters spend 15–30 minutes per application copying deposits and balances from PDF statements into a worksheet.
What the demo does
Extracts every transaction, groups recurring deposits as likely income, checks that running balances reconcile, and flags pages it could not read.
Built with
Python, FastAPI, OCR, a large language model for layout understanding, validation rules, PostgreSQL.

Demo · synthetic dataInvestigation

Alert triage and case narrative assistant

Ranks transaction-monitoring alerts and drafts a narrative an analyst can review, edit and approve.

The problem
Small BSA teams face growing alert queues, and much of each investigation is gathering transactions and writing up what they show.
What the demo does
Scores each alert against configurable patterns, explains the score in plain language, and writes a first-draft narrative where every sentence links to its evidence.
Built with
Python, rules engine, large language model with structured output, audit logging, review interface in Next.js.

Demo · public documentsKnowledge assistant

Procedure assistant with cited answers

Answers staff questions from a library of procedures and public regulatory guidance, always showing where the answer came from.

The problem
Front-line staff interrupt supervisors or search long PDFs for answers that already exist in the procedure manual.
What the demo does
Searches the document library, answers in two or three sentences with page citations, and says so when the documents do not cover the question.
Built with
Retrieval-augmented generation, hybrid search, PostgreSQL with vector search, document refresh pipeline.

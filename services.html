Services

We build AI for one job: reading financial documents well.

Four services, one specialty. If your team spends hours each week reading statements, applications, alerts or policies, one of these will fit, and we will tell you plainly if none do.

01

Document data extraction

Turn PDFs, scans and photos into clean, checked data your systems can use, with low-confidence values sent to a person instead of guessed.

What's included

  • Field and table extraction from statements, pay stubs, invoices and tax forms
  • Handling for scans, phone photos and multi-page files
  • Validation rules: totals that add up, dates in range, required fields
  • Review queue for anything below your confidence threshold
  • Export to CSV, Excel, your core system or an API
  • Accuracy report on your own sample set
Typical use: a lending team receives 300 bank statements a month and keys income and balances by hand. Extraction fills a review screen with every value pre-filled and linked to its page, and the analyst confirms instead of typing.
02

Investigation & case assistants

Help BSA/AML, fraud and dispute teams clear queues faster, with findings an examiner can follow from claim to source.

What's included

  • Alert triage that ranks cases by risk and explains why
  • Pattern checks: structuring, rapid movement, new beneficiaries
  • Draft case narratives in your team's format
  • Every statement linked to the transaction or page behind it
  • Analyst approve, edit and reject controls with full audit log
  • Configurable rules your compliance team owns
Typical use: an analyst opens an alert and finds the relevant transactions already pulled, the pattern described and a first-draft narrative written. They check the evidence, edit the wording and decide, and the AI never files anything on its own.
03

Policy & knowledge assistants

Give staff one place to ask questions of your procedures, product guides and regulatory references, with answers that cite the exact source.

What's included

  • Search across PDFs, Word files, wikis and shared drives
  • Answers with page-level citations, or "I don't know"
  • Access rules that respect who may see which documents
  • Automatic refresh when a policy document changes
  • Usage and gap reports: which questions go unanswered
  • Web, Teams or Slack front end
Typical use: a new member-service rep asks "What ID do we accept for a joint account with a minor?" and gets a two-line answer with a link to section 4.2 of the account-opening procedure.
04

Workflow automation & integrations

Connect AI to the tools you already use, so documents arrive, get processed and land in the right queue without someone moving them.

What's included

  • Email and upload intake with automatic sorting
  • Integrations with storage, case management and core systems
  • MCP servers and tool connections for AI agents
  • Approval steps wherever money or customers are affected
  • Monitoring, alerts and usage-cost tracking
  • Documentation your IT team can maintain
Typical use: loan documents emailed by applicants are read, sorted by type, checked for missing pages and filed to the right loan record, and the loan officer receives one message listing what is still outstanding.

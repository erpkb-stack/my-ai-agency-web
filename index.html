How we work

Built for teams that answer to examiners.

  • Your data stays yoursWe work in your cloud or ours under a signed NDA, never train public models on your documents, and use test data until you approve otherwise.
  • Every answer shows its sourceExtracted values and drafted findings link back to the page and line they came from, so reviewers can check them in seconds.
  • People approve the decisionsThe AI drafts and flags. Your staff approve, edit or reject, and every action is logged.
  • Measured before it shipsWe agree on accuracy targets up front and test against them on your real documents before anything goes live.
